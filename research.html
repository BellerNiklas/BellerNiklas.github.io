Research

My interests are in macroeconomics, monetary economics, fiscal policy, and macro-finance, with a particular interest in how policy shocks shape expectations, asset prices, and household outcomes.

Current Project

Fiscal Policy and Household Inflation Expectations during Germany's Energy Crisis (Master's Thesis)

University of Bonn

Using Bundesbank BOP-HH microdata, I study how Germany's 2022-23 energy-crisis fiscal measures shaped household inflation expectations, with a focus on announcement versus realized-bill effects and heterogeneity by energy exposure and policy knowledge.
