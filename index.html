Niklas Beller

Student in Economics

I am a Master's student in Economics at the University of Bonn and completed an exchange semester at ENSAE Paris. My interests are in macroeconomics, monetary economics, fiscal policy, and macro-finance.

I have worked as a research assistant on projects on inflation surprises and asset returns, fiscal rules and labor markets, and capital gains taxation. Before Bonn, I studied Economics at the University of Mannheim. I am a merit-based scholarship recipient of the Friedrich-Ebert-Foundation.
