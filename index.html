About

I am a Master's student in Economics at the University of Bonn. My academic focus is on quantitative and empirical methods in economics, with particular interests in macroeconomics, monetary economics, labor economics, and public policy.

I have research experience working with leading economists on topics ranging from inflation dynamics and asset returns to capital gains taxation and household finance. My work combines rigorous economic theory with advanced econometric methods and large-scale data analysis.

Prior to my Master's studies, I completed my Bachelor's degree in Economics at the University of Mannheim. I am a merit-based scholarship recipient of the Friedrich-Ebert-Foundation.
