  \resumeSubHeadingListStart
    \resumeProjectHeading
      {Can Monetary Policy Move Household Expectations? Evidence from the ECB's July 2022 Policy Turn (Master's Thesis)}{}
      \resumeItemListStart
        \resumeItem{Uses daily interview timing in Wave 31 of the Bundesbank Online Panel Households to compare German respondents surveyed immediately before and after the ECB's 21 July 2022 decision to raise rates by 50 basis points.}
        \resumeItem{Finds one-year inflation expectations 0.53 percentage points lower after the decision, driven by a shift from extreme inflation scenarios toward the 4--8\% range; expected lending and savings rates rose, suggesting that a salient policy turn can moderate perceived near-term inflation risks.}
      \resumeItemListEnd

      \resumeItemListStart
        \resumeItem{Built a large monthly macro-financial database for 19 euro-area countries, combining more than 1,700 variables from Eurostat, ECB, OECD, and BIS into cleaned country-level and euro-area panels for uncertainty measurement.}
